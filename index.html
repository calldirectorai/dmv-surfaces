01 / About Us

Delivering Flooring Solutions with Precision

DMV Surfaces is a full-service commercial flooring and consulting company headquartered in Ashburn, VA. Leveraging over 30 years of combined industry experience, Arash Moazzez founded the company with a commitment to quality craftsmanship, reliable service, and delivering exceptional results on projects of any size.

We specialize in strategic consulting and certified independent flooring inspections, bridging the gap between design intent and real-world performance. Our services help clients ensure quality installations, resolve disputes, and achieve long-term flooring success through expert guidance and unbiased assessments.

02 / Our Services

What We Do

Wall to wall and floor to ceiling surface solutions from specification to preparation.

Specification & Consulting

Independent product specification, warranty guidance, and project planning.

Estimating & Planning

Accurate budgeting, material takeoffs, and lifecycle cost analysis.
